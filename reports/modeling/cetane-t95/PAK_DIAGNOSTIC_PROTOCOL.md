Initial results revealed missing fresh laboratory density as the main loss of
cetane predictor coverage in 2025/2026. This follow-up is explicitly post hoc,
not part of the frozen candidate selection or a promotion criterion.

Evaluate the unmodified D976 equation with the last PAK D15 available at origin
(age <=30 minutes, density700–1000 kg/m3) and the last published same-point
laboratory T50 (sample+4h, sample age<=48h, 100–450 C). No fitting, offset,
lag search, threshold tuning or claim that PAK is a reference density measurement.

CN<51 and false alarms, plus persistence on the same overlap.
